Made for surf schools

Weather cancels lessons. Not your bookings.

When the surf or weather forces a cancellation, keeping track of everyone who needs a new lesson can get complicated. Atlantic Rebook is a simple concept for helping surf schools organise cancelled bookings, find new session spaces and keep track of revenue at risk.

A student project for surf schools · Atlantic Edge Surf School, Lahinch

A simpler workflow

Three steps. One clearer picture.

A proposed way to manage the work that starts after a lesson gets cancelled.

01

Cancel

Mark a cancelled session and identify the bookings that need a new lesson.

02

Rebook

Review suitable upcoming sessions and check that there is enough space for each group.

03

Track

See which bookings are still waiting and estimate the value associated with those people.
