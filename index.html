Weather cancels lessons. Not your bookings.

The Atlantic doesn't always play ball. When a lesson gets cancelled, there's still a group of people waiting to surf. Atlantic Rebook explores a simpler way to organise those bookings, find new session spaces and see what's still outstanding.

01 / The idea

Less chasing.
More getting back in the water.

A cancellation creates a second job: working out who needs a new slot, where the space is and which bookings still need attention. Atlantic Rebook is a prototype exploring that workflow.

01 ↘

Cancel

Mark the affected session and bring its bookings into one place for follow-up.

02 ↗

Rebook

Check upcoming sessions and match each group to a slot with enough free places.

03 ◎

Track

See which groups are still waiting and estimate the value associated with those bookings.
