Project notes

Behind the build

Atlantic Rebook is a student project exploring how surf schools could manage cancelled lessons and arrange replacement bookings. These notes explain the idea, its current limitations and the testing work behind the prototype.

Project purpose

Surf lessons can be cancelled because of weather or sea conditions. A cancellation may leave a school needing to contact several customers, find enough places in other sessions and keep track of bookings that are still unresolved.

Atlantic Rebook explores a simple workflow built around three actions: cancel a session, rebook the affected groups and track outstanding bookings and their associated value.

The intended audience is surf schools, with Atlantic Edge Surf School in Lahinch, County Clare, providing the project context. The concept and proposed price are not evidence of market demand.

Current limitations

  • The landing page is a static website and does not provide a working customer registration service.
  • The interest form only displays a local thank-you message. It does not collect or transmit submissions.
  • The separate demo is a prototype and is not a shared, multi-user booking management system.
  • The proposed price of €19 per month has not been validated. It is an initial pricing idea, not a live offer.
  • Sample bookings, names and contact details in the prototype are fictional. They must not be treated as real customer records.
  • No claim is made that the project has been tested by real surf schools or that it produces a measured business outcome.

AI assistance

AI assistance was used to help draft the HTML, CSS and JavaScript, structure the landing page, develop responsive styling and document the prototype's limitations.

The generated code should be reviewed and tested by the project author before publication. The author is responsible for checking that the published website works as intended and accurately represents the project.

No external JavaScript libraries are required by these two pages. The interest form does not store or transmit visitor information.
