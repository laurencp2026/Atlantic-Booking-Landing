Field notes · Lahinch, Ireland

What sits behind the prototype.

Research, assumptions and testing notes for Atlantic Rebook — a concept for helping a small surf school organise weather-cancelled lessons and the bookings that need attention afterwards.

01 / The problem

When the sea says no.

Surf lessons can be cancelled when weather or sea conditions are unsuitable. For a small surf school, each cancellation can create follow-up work: finding affected bookings, identifying alternative sessions, contacting customers and keeping track of revenue still at risk.

Atlantic Rebook explores whether a focused operations view can make the cancellation and rebooking workload easier to organise.

03 / Lean product requirements

What the prototype is testing

Primary user

Owner or manager of a small surf school who needs to manage cancelled lessons and affected bookings.

Core hypothesis

A dedicated cancellation queue, alternative-session view and money-at-risk estimate can reduce the effort needed to keep track of affected bookings.

Primary action

Review affected bookings and select a suitable alternative session to propose to the customer.

Success signal

Interest from surf-school operators and evidence that the workflow makes it faster or clearer to organise cancellations.

Out of scope for this prototype: online payments, automatic customer messages, live forecast integrations, production booking-system integrations and a shared multi-user database.

04 / Business model assumption

A small monthly subscription?

One initial pricing hypothesis is €19 per month per surf school. The idea is that a small operator may pay for a tool that reduces cancellation administration and helps keep affected bookings visible.

Not validated yet. The price and willingness to pay are assumptions to test through conversations with surf-school owners. The prototype does not prove demand.
05 / Prototype limitations

What this version does — and does not do

Rebooking limitation: Atlantic Rebook helps the owner organise cancelled bookings and select alternative sessions. It does not automatically contact customers or confirm that a proposed time suits them. The owner must contact the customer separately to agree the new time.

  • The records and weather values are fictional sample data.
  • Changes are stored in the current browser using local storage; there is no shared database.
  • The weather examples are illustrative only, not live forecasts or safety advice.
  • The prototype does not send email or SMS messages, take payments or connect to an existing booking system.
  • The displayed money-at-risk figure is an estimate based on sample bookings and an assumed lesson price, not actual revenue.
06 / Use of AI assistance

How AI supported the work

AI assistance was used to help draft and refine interface copy, explore the workflow, generate and revise HTML/CSS/JavaScript, and identify prototype limitations and testing ideas. The output was reviewed and adjusted as part of the project. Research assumptions, sample data and prototype behaviour should still be checked by the project author.
